Penny's Seaside Fun

1. Penny stretches her flippers on a sandy beach, surrounded by seagulls flying above.
2. Penny waddles towards a friendly seagull perched on a nearby rock, who waves at her.
3. Penny searches through the sand, collecting seashells and placing them in her nest.
4. Penny slips into the calm water, her flippers paddling as she swims towards a school of fish.
5. Penny returns to her nest, snuggles into her cozy bed of seashells, and closes her eyes.
