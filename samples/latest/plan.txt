Penny's Seaside Adventure

1. Penny lies in her cozy nest, surrounded by seashells and pebbles.
2. Penny waddles down to the beach, her flippers flapping in excitement.
3. Penny meets a friendly seagull perched on a rock, watching her play.
4. Penny discovers a shiny shell buried in the sand, her eyes shining with excitement.
5. Penny waddles back to her nest, tired but happy, with her shell in her flippers.
